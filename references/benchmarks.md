# Benchmarks

Checked on November 19, 2026

This page gives market reference points to position an app. It is not part of the spec: its figures change every quarter, while AREA's rules stay stable from one version to the next.

## How to read them

1. **A benchmark positions, it does not judge.** A figure above or below average does not say whether the tracking is good, or whether the product is doing well. It helps ask a question, not give a verdict.
2. **Compare with your category.** A D30 retention of 3% is average for a shopping app, but low for a news app.
3. **Benchmarks measure app opens, since install.** They are therefore compared with [open retention](../spec/pillars/retention.md#secondary-kpis), never with core retention or with the activated cohort. Two gaps remain: AREA's open retention does not count installs that were never opened, and it only contains users who accepted measurement, who are often more engaged than the others. Both make it look higher than the benchmark.
4. **Sources do not agree with each other.** Scope, calculation method (average per app or across all users) and period vary from one publisher to another. Keep a single source to compare over time.
5. **Figures age.** Each table states its period and the date it was checked.

## Open retention by category

Source: [AppsFlyer benchmarks tool](https://www.appsflyer.com/benchmarks/), Q3 2026 data, collected on November 17, 2026. Filters: worldwide, all app sizes, iOS and Android combined, all acquisition channels. Share of users who open the app 1, 7 or 30 days after install.

The statistic is an **average per app**: each app counts the same, whatever its size, and the top 10% and bottom 10% of apps are removed before the calculation. It is neither a median nor the retention of all users combined.

| Sports | 25.6% | 14.6% | 6.0% |

| Finance | 22.3% | 10.6% | 4.8% |

| Shopping | 19.4% | 8.6% | 3.3% |
| Social | 22.9% | 8.0% | 2.7% |

| Games | 25.2% | 7.3% | 2.0% |

The Health and fitness and Shopping rows are not a copying error: their values differ only in the second decimal (19.43% against 19.42% at D1, 8.57% against 8.55% at D7, 3.30% in both cases at D30).

Across the tool's 25 categories, D30 retention ranges from 0.9% to 9.1%.

## What has no benchmark yet

No public source measures core retention, the activation rate or the core usage rate as AREA defines them. For these KPIs, the best reference is still the app's own trend, from one cohort to the next. A benchmark called "activation" at a publisher does not necessarily measure the same thing as AREA: check its definition before any comparison.
